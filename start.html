For student leaders

Start your own chapter

You do not need experience. You need a few friends, some local businesses, and this playbook.

Talk to us ↗

Why it works

Small businesses often have no time or money for a website. Students have the skills and the free time. Matching the two helps everyone, and it costs the business nothing.

  • Free for businesses

    A community program, not a company.

  • Real experience for students

    Design, writing, and web skills on real projects.

What you need

  • 3 to 6 students who want to help
  • A teacher or adult who can advise
  • A list of local businesses to talk to
  • A simple website host and a shared inbox

Roles on a team

Web designers, photographers, designers, and communications. One person can do more than one.

The playbook

Five steps. Do them in order, write down what you learn, and hand it to the next group of students.

  1. 1

    Meet your neighbors

    Visit shops, restaurants, and services near you. Ask what they need online. Listen first.

  2. 2

    Build your team

    Find students for each role and agree who talks to businesses.

  3. 3

    Start with one business

    Do one project well before you take on more. Check every detail with the owner.

  4. 4

    Keep it running

    Give each business a way to ask for changes, and say when they will be done.

  5. 5

    Pass it on

    Train younger students and write down what you learned, so the chapter lasts after you graduate.

Your first 30 days

  1. Week 1: pick your team and an adviser.
  2. Week 2: visit five businesses.
  3. Week 3: choose one business and agree on the work.
  4. Week 4: share a first draft and ask for feedback.

Things to remember

  • Respect each owner's language and culture.
  • Never promise what you cannot finish.
  • Keep business information private.
  • Ask for help early.
